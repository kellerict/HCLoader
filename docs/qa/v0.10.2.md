- #22 `Parking info: see website` -> artist.

In the other 25 pastes all junk lines (hashtags, CTAs, «Doors open», «Buy tickets», «Share this», «Check the app», Dutch «Bijna uitverkocht») were skipped.

## Findings (for the developer)
1. «Label: sentence» info lines (`Parking info: see website`, `Sponsored by X`, `Last entry HH:MM`) are not recognised as free text; `Last entry 21:30` even gets a time. Suggest a stoplist for info nouns (entry, parking, sponsored, info, cloakroom, curfew) and for a leading «entry/entrée/entrada».
2. French marketing (`Entrée gratuite avant 17h`) is not covered; check other languages for the prose heuristic (sentence with a time-of-day word at the end).
3. Single-word stage lines in app cards and ALL-CAPS unknown areas are indistinguishable from artists without a catalog; consider reporting such lines in `skipped`/ask the user rather than importing as artists, or make the stage detection use «line is followed by a name line and preceded by a time line».
4. `Bühne X` / `Scène X` keep the prefix in the stage name (cosmetic).
