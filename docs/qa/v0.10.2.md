## Findings (for the developer)
1. «Label: sentence» info lines (`Parking info: see website`, `Sponsored by X`, `Last entry HH:MM`) are not recognised as free text; `Last entry 21:30` even gets a time. Suggest a stoplist for info nouns (entry, parking, sponsored, info, cloakroom, curfew) and for a leading «entry/entrée/entrada».
2. French marketing (`Entrée gratuite avant 17h`) is not covered; check other languages for the prose heuristic (sentence with a time-of-day word at the end).
3. Single-word stage lines in app cards and ALL-CAPS unknown areas are indistinguishable from artists without a catalog; consider reporting such lines in `skipped`/ask the user rather than importing as artists, or make the stage detection use «line is followed by a name line and preceded by a time line».
4. `Bühne X` / `Scène X` keep the prefix in the stage name (cosmetic).

## Failing families (for the developer)
1. Free-text / greeting / forward lines without time inside a stage context become artists: «Forwarded from», «Hi hardcore family», «The Team», «Beste ravers», `Entradas…`, `Biglietti…`, `Harmonogram – sobota`, WhatsApp chat lines, `📍 venue`, `TBA`, `Gates open 16:00`. Multilingual prose heuristic is too narrow; consider rejecting untimed lines in timed layouts and an info-noun stoplist (gates, entradas, biglietti, wstęp, forwarded, tba/tbc).
2. Day/stage lost when contained in a sentence or modified heading: `…line-up for Saturday:`, `Sunday schedule`, `Zeitplan Freitag, 04.07.`, `PROGRAMMA DOMENICA`, `Harmonogram – sobota`, `hosted by`, emoji-only stage heading.
3. Time syntax: backticked `` `14:00` ``, `HH:MM TZ / HH:MM TZ`, `DAY → HH:MM`, `Main: 18:00 A, 19:00 B` after a `DAY n` heading, `Name (13:00-14:00)` with a bullet and stage heading containing parentheses.
4. Broken line wraps (`A &` / `B`) are not rejoined.
5. MC credits (`ft./feat./w/ MC X`) stay inside the artist name.
